# Workflow basis

Reviewed September 26, 2026. These references inform demonstration behavior, not a claim that the application satisfies every contract, product standard or jurisdiction.

| Primary source | Application decision |
|---|---|
| [NPCA Quality Control Manual, 17th edition, February 2024](https://precast.org/wp-content/uploads/020124_QualityControlManual_17th_Edition.pdf), §§4.6–4.8 and Appendix B | Release/stripping strength and shipping strength are separate project-specific requirements. A timer does not release a piece. Repairs retain their disposition and inspection history. |
| [FHWA, Acceptance of Non-Structural Precast Elements, HIF-13-045, June 2013](https://www.fhwa.dot.gov/pavement/materials/hif13045.pdf), pp. 2–5 | Record pre-pour checks, batch/test references, curing information, post-pour inspection and disposition. This guidance concerns non-structural precast acceptance and is not a universal structural specification. |
| [TxDOT Concrete Repair Manual, repair submission and approval](https://www.txdot.gov/manuals/brg/crm/chapter-1--introduction/section-5--repair-procedure-submission-and-approva.html) | Keep the defect, approved procedure/reference, repair execution, and reinspection as distinct records. Project/engineer approval requirements remain authoritative. |
| [PCI certification overview](https://www.pci.org/certification) | Certification concerns plant, personnel and erection programs. Software use does not confer PCI certification. |

Production sequence: engineering release → formwork readiness → pre-pour QC → placement → curing → release-strength verification → stripping → post-pour/final QC → yard → loading → departure → receipt.

Hold state is independent of the physical production stage. A defect can occur at any stage. Repair completion does not imply acceptance; QC Manager review clears each repaired defect, and the piece remains on hold until every outstanding defect is accepted. Held stock cannot advance or ship. Receiving exceptions are recorded independently from erection or installation.

PrecastFlow's default strength numbers, mixes, projects and people are fictional examples. The application records evidence and compares entered measurements against configured thresholds; it does not determine the engineering requirements itself.
